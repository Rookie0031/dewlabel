Dewlabel Support

Dewlabel reads the ingredient list (전성분) on a Korean cosmetics label from a photo and checks it against your skin profile. The text is read on your iPhone.

Contact

Questions and bug reports: open an issue.

Getting a good result

  • Fill the frame with the 전성분 section. Small print reads better closer up.
  • If only a few ingredients could be checked, the result says so and shows no score. That scan does not count toward your free scans.
  • The verdict uses only ingredients in Dewlabel's database. The result shows how many it was based on.

Common questions

What is free? Three scans a week (a new week starts Monday at midnight, your iPhone's time) and up to ten products on the shelf. Dewlabel Pro removes both limits. It is a yearly or monthly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase.

How do I cancel? Settings › Apple Account › Subscriptions. Use "Restore Purchases" in the app on another iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account.

Is this medical advice? No. Dewlabel gives information about ingredients. Patch test new products and ask a doctor about skin conditions or pregnancy.

Requires iOS 26 or later.
