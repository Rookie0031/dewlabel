Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Dewlabel does not collect personal data, send it to a server, or share it. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

  • Label photos. The text on a photo is read on your iPhone. Dewlabel does not upload photos and does not keep them after reading.
  • Your skin profile (skin type, sensitive, acne-prone, avoiding fragrance, pregnant or breastfeeding). Stored only on this iPhone and used only to check labels.
  • Your shelf. For each product you save: the name you give it, the label text that was read, and the result. Stored only on this iPhone.
  • Usage counts. How many scans you did this week (for the free limit) and simple counts of scans, paywall views and purchases. They contain no product names, ingredients or identifiers and are not sent anywhere.

Explanations

On devices with Apple Intelligence, the plain-language explanation is written by Apple's on-device model. Nothing is sent to us or to any other server for it.

Camera

Dewlabel asks for the camera only to take a photo of an ingredient list.

Purchases

Dewlabel Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple processes the payment. Dewlabel only checks with StoreKit whether Pro is active on your Apple Account. We never see your payment details.

Deleting your data

Delete a product by swiping it on the shelf. Deleting the app removes your profile, shelf and counts.
